# Architecture Knowledge and Learning

## Status

**Approved direction; future capability.** The discussion explicitly approves verified knowledge, interactive walkthroughs, learning paths, sharing, and freshness tracking. This is distinct from codebase explanations.

## Purpose

Help developers learn reusable engineering practices and understand a system through reviewed material connected to architecture and implementation.

## Responsibilities

- Organize architecture guides, engineering practices, patterns, and technical explanations.
- Provide walkthroughs of components, dependencies, and data flows.
- Offer learning paths by topic, technology, or skill level.
- Allow teams to share reviewed knowledge and maintain team knowledge bases.
- Surface relevant material while a developer explores graph entities.
- Identify poorly documented areas and potentially stale documentation.
- Support maintainer review and freshness status.

## Trust classes

Maintain distinct states for verified/approved knowledge, imported material, AI-generated explanations, and unreviewed community contributions. Do not make a contribution authoritative merely because it is linked to a graph node.

## Acceptance evidence

- Knowledge entries retain source, owner/contributor, review, and freshness metadata.
- A walkthrough links steps to current graph entities and source evidence.
- Stale references are detectable after graph changes.
- Review actions do not alter observed implementation facts.

## Boundary

This capability curates reusable learning content. The [Developer Knowledge Intelligence](developer-knowledge-intelligence.md) capability answers repository-specific understanding questions.
