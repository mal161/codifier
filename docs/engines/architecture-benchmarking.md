# Architecture Benchmarking

## Status

**Approval trail is ambiguous; reconfirm before implementation.** Benchmarking is listed in the decision register as a future direction, with a note that the surrounding approval exchange is irregular.

## Purpose

Compare an architecture against a selected standard, reference architecture, organization-authored rule set, or historical baseline and provide evidence-backed gaps and recommendations.

## Responsibilities

- Identify the benchmark source, version, scope, and applicability.
- Evaluate only criteria that can be assessed from available graph evidence.
- Report evidence, unknowns, exceptions, and criteria not assessed.
- Compare with a previous revision when the scope and rule versions are compatible.
- Offer actionable recommendations as proposals, not automatic architecture changes.

## Rules

- Avoid universal rankings or scores that hide context.
- Keep organization rules and external standards versioned and attributable.
- Separate measured findings from subjective interpretation or AI commentary.
- Never claim that a benchmark proves legal or regulatory compliance.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every result identifies benchmark/rule version and applicable graph revision.
- Each gap links to supporting evidence and affected entities.
- Unsupported criteria are shown as unknown/not assessed.
- Results do not alter intended or actual architecture automatically.

## Decision gate

Before implementation, clarify the source of benchmarks, licensing, ownership, update process, target users, and relationship to compliance assessments.
